How to use this LitBuy spreadsheet

A spreadsheet is a research tool. Keep the exact listing, option and unanswered questions together so a saved photograph becomes a useful comparison.

1

Discover & record

Open a CAFANS reference. Save the source ID, exact size, color and quantity. Check whether the listing covers one piece or a complete set.

2

Confirm before payment

If choosing LitBuy, check its current buying options and item eligibility. Confirm the original listing, price, domestic delivery and applicable return conditions.

3

Review before shipping

Compare your own order photos with the selected option. Resolve visible mismatches and confirm the international shipping quote before approving dispatch.

Fit before labels

Make size comparisons useful.

A size letter or number is only a starting point. Use measurements from the exact listing and keep the measurement method consistent.

  • T-shirts and sweatshirts: compare laid-flat chest width, shoulder width, length and sleeves with a garment you own.
  • Shoes and slides: record the stated sizing system; distinguish foot length from insole or footbed length.
  • Every option: save the color, size and quantity together. Ask about missing measurements before ordering.
Your order, your evidence

A practical QC checklist.

Product listing photos show the advertised item. Warehouse photos should relate to the item actually received for your order.

  • Match the item, color, size label and included pieces to the order record.
  • Inspect visible seams, print placement, fastenings and obvious damage.
  • Request a clear close-up or measurement when an important detail is unclear.
  • Record issues promptly and check the order’s applicable return window.

Photographs alone cannot establish authenticity, composition or long-term durability.

Budget beyond the item

Estimate the complete cost.

Use one currency for every entry. Add your quoted item subtotal, domestic delivery, international shipping and other expected charges.

This tool only adds your inputs. It does not obtain shipping quotes, calculate tax rules or predict exchange rates. Leave unknown costs under review instead of treating the result as a final quote.

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list used to organize finds for possible purchasing through LitBuy. Useful records include source links, selected options, measurements, prices and inspection notes. This page provides a small CAFANS catalog selection and an independent buying checklist.

Where do the buttons take me?

The “LitBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons both open https://cafans.com. Individual product links open the corresponding CAFANS detail page. This page does not operate a checkout.

Is this an official LitBuy website?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. References to LitBuy describe the intended research workflow and do not imply an official affiliation, endorsement or guarantee.

Are these real CAFANS products or example listings?

The three entries reproduce product names and images from CAFANS pages, with their original source IDs and direct links. They are catalog references, not proof of stock, product quality or successful purchase. Check the current source before ordering.

Can every listed item be purchased through LitBuy?

That is not guaranteed. Check the platform’s current acceptance rules and whether the exact source link is supported before paying. Availability and purchasing restrictions may vary by item and destination.

Are product prices and shipping updated automatically?

No. This static selection directs you to the source for current pricing and availability. Shipping depends on the actual parcel, route, destination and applicable services. Use a current quote when planning your budget.

How can I download my shortlist?

Select “Save” on the items you want, then choose “Export shortlist CSV.” The exported file contains product names, categories and CAFANS source links, with blank fields for your size, color, quoted price and notes. Selections remain only for the current page visit.

Does a listing photograph count as a QC photo?

No. A listing photograph illustrates the seller’s advertised product. For an actual order, review the inspection evidence associated with your received item and ask about anything unclear before approving shipment.
